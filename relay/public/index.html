Share your drop tables

The more players send their tables, the more accurate everyone's drop chances get. You don't need an account.

  1. In game, click in the Loot Finder or at the bottom of the drop table window. /dt export and Share my tables in the minimap button's right-click menu open the same window.
  2. Press Select and then Ctrl+C.
  3. Paste the text into the box and press Send.
A big table comes out in several parts. Send each one by itself, and press Next part in the game for the next one.

It carries mob and item ids, kill and drop counts, levels, the zones of your kills and a random id of your addon install, never character or account names. Anything sent here is public and is published under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license, credited to the Drop Tables contributors.
